Luma has joined Human After All, Webedia-Elephant's new AI Creator Studio launching in Paris, partnering with Google and ElevenLabs to provide creators with AI tools for film production. The studio emphasizes keeping human creativity at the center while using AI to expand creative possibilities, such as real-time performance transformation and video-to-video consistency without traditional production requirements. The initiative demonstrates how AI can augment rather than replace human creators throughout the production process.
