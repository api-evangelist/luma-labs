Luma is launching an open science laboratory focused on solving generalization problems in physical AI and robotics, aiming to democratize access to physical AI infrastructure. The company plans to collaborate with academia and industry partners to develop world models that enable robots to operate effectively across diverse tasks and environments. The initiative is designed to prevent a few companies from controlling this transformative technology by making physical AI infrastructure broadly accessible.
